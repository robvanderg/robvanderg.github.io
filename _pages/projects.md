Projects
----------------------

### 3CPO: Comprehensive Cross-lingual Contextual Pretraining \& Optimization for Language Models

This project proposes to train language models with language diversity as a first priority. We 
adapt the data selection, tokenizer, and evaluation to reflect diversity in all aspects of
model development. 

Funded by: Nvidia Academic Grant Programme

### LMLM: Linguistically Motivated Language Models

LMLM proposes to build Linguistically Motivated LLMs. This is done 
by adapting the training regime as well as the LLM architecture to
incorporate different linguistic levels. This will lead to mitigation
of shortcut learning and more reliable LLMs.


Funded by: [Carlsberg Semper Ardens: Accelerate](https://www.carlsbergfondet.dk/det-har-vi-stoettet/cf25-1861/)
